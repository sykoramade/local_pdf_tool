## Free → Pro Conversion (The Real Lever)

**Current:** 1-2% (unlimited free = no reason to upgrade)

**Sprint 20 A/B Test:** Limit free annotations to 5/doc → expect 2-4% conversion

**Sprint 21+:** Full freemium model → expect 2-5% conversion

**Impact:** At 500 free users, freemium could generate 10-25 additional Pro conversions/mo = +$90-225/mo revenue
